02 — PHASE 01DISCOVER THE CHALLENGE

Every river tells
a bigger story.

Our starting point is a question: what pressures could be affecting water quality in the Otún River basin?

Our project explores how solid waste and untreated discharges may affect the river basin. It also examines waste-collection access and how discharges are managed. These questions require reliable local evidence before conclusions can be drawn. Some changes may have more than one cause, so observations should be checked by qualified staff before being shared as fact.

“
OUR WORKING HYPOTHESIS

If communities and local decision-makers improve waste collection and discharge oversight, less waste could enter waterways, because prevention and shared responsibility may reduce pollution pressures.

A testable starting point—not a measured result.

READING THE RESULTS

What stands out?

Most respondents selected multiple listed pollution causes and supported combined conservation actions. Views on whether better waste collection would reduce severe pollution were divided.

These answers reflect a small group’s opinions. They cannot confirm pollution sources, river conditions, or what the wider basin community believes. Environmental claims require independent monitoring and further local research.
